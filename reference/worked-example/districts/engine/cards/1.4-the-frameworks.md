# 1.4 · The Frameworks

**Type + State:** ENGINE · LIVE — the routing table names `frameworks/buyer-fit.md` for Matching
and `frameworks/pricing.md` for Pricing (`rules.md:28-29`), and `frameworks/international-buyers.md`
in the column for a foreign buyer (`:25`, `:27-28`). The one market name in them
(`international-buyers.md:32`, Jerusalem) is an illustration, not a branch.

**What it is:** the method behind the judgement jobs — how a buyer is scored against a listing, how
a price band is built, how a foreign buyer changes both. Each file opens with what it depends on
and who calls it.

**Why it is shaped that way:** one method has to serve every market, so the method lives here and
the numbers live in the market. `frameworks/pricing.md:7` says it outright: adjustment
*categories* are universal (this file), adjustment *magnitudes* are local (`region/market.md`).

**Source path:** `specialist/reference/frameworks/`

**Hits:** every market that runs Matching or Pricing, immediately. `international-buyers.md` is
also called from inside the other two and from the listing template, so it reaches all three
judgement jobs.

**Does not hit:** the numbers. A reader who thinks a band is too wide reaches for `pricing.md`; the
magnitudes it applies are in each market's `region/market.md`, and an edit here changes no figure.

**Done when:** you can say whether your change is to the method (here) or to a market's numbers
(card 04's slots), and you have opened at most the one framework you mean. Stop there.
